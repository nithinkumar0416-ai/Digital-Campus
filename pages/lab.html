✅ Recommended Safe Measurement Sequence
  1. Step 1: Power Down: Switch off DC bench supply and discharge capacitor with a 1k resistor.
  2. Step 2: Probe Ground Check: Verify oscilloscope black alligator clip is physically clipped to circuit ground rail.
  3. Step 3: Breadboard Rail Continuity: With power OFF, set DMM to continuity/resistance mode. Verify breadboard power rails are not split in the middle.
  4. Step 4: Capacitor Polarity Check: Ensure negative stripe on electrolytic capacitor is facing ground.
  5. Step 5: When to Call Lab Instructor: If component feels warm to touch or DMM reads internal power supply short circuit, immediately call Dr. Dev Kumar or the lab technician.
